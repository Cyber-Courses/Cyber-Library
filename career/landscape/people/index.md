# People

Cybersecurity is shaped by the people who research, build, defend, teach, and organize within it. More than any tool or vendor, individuals and the groups they form set the agenda for how defenders think, and reputation and knowledge travel through them. Knowing whose work to follow and which **communities** to join keeps a professional oriented amid constant change.

Influence comes from many corners: academic researchers, incident responders, open-source maintainers, standards contributors, and writers who explain the field clearly. No single list captures them, and standing shifts as new work earns attention, so the quality and integrity of a contribution matter more than visibility alone.

## In this section

- [Academia](academia.md): universities, researchers, and the peer-reviewed venues that generate foundational security knowledge.
- [Community](community.md): volunteer-driven events, forums, and open-source projects where practitioners connect outside any single employer.
- [Industry](industry.md): the commercial practitioners and influential voices inside vendors, service firms, and enterprises.
- [Public](public.md): the government agencies and officials that set policy, defend infrastructure, and publish authoritative guidance.
