# GRC Program Manager

Security governance tends to fragment across teams and tools, and someone has to keep the pieces aligned, moving, and visible to leadership. That is the GRC program manager's job. Rather than performing any single analysis, this role designs and runs the **program** that connects policy, risk treatment, and regulatory obligations into one coherent effort with clear ownership, schedules, and reporting. It is a coordinating and accountable role more than a hands-on investigative one.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain the catalog of controls and the frameworks the organization follows.
- Plan and sequence assessments and audits so they do not collide.
- Translate risk findings and compliance gaps into prioritized remediation plans and track them to closure.
- Manage the calendar of recurring obligations such as attestations and reviews.
- Prepare accurate, timely summaries that let executives and boards understand where the program stands.

## Skills and background

- **Breadth over depth:** a working understanding of common frameworks and regulations across the GRC disciplines rather than deep specialization in one.
- **Evaluation fluency:** familiarity with how risk is assessed and controls are evaluated.
- **Project management:** the skill to drive work across teams that do not report to them.
- **Influence and facilitation:** strong writing, calm facilitation, and the trust-building that lets the role function on influence rather than authority.

## Getting into the role

People commonly enter after time as a risk analyst, compliance analyst, or auditor, where they learn the substance of the work before taking responsibility for coordinating it. Others arrive from project or program management backgrounds and build their domain knowledge on the job, often supported by certifications in security management or audit.

## Career progression

With experience, a program manager may grow into leadership of the wider GRC function, into roles such as head of risk or compliance, or toward the chief information security officer track.

## Related roles

- [Risk Analyst](risk-analyst.md): produces the assessments the program schedules and reports on.
- [Compliance Analyst](compliance-analyst.md): produces the control evidence the program coordinates and tracks.
- [Policy Analyst](policy-analyst.md): drafts the governance documents the program operationalizes.
- [Security Auditor](security-auditor.md): runs the reviews the program coordinates and whose findings it drives to resolution.
