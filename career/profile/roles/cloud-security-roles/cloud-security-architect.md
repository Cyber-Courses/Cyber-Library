# Cloud Security Architect

A **cloud security architect** defines how an organization's cloud environments should be built, protected, and governed. Rather than responding to individual incidents or configuring single systems, the architect sets the structure within which others work: the **reference designs**, controls, and guardrails that shape how applications and data live across a public cloud provider or a hybrid estate. The role is distinguished by its breadth and its altitude, reasoning about an entire environment and the trade-offs that run through it.

## Responsibilities

- Translate business and regulatory requirements into security patterns for identity and access management, network segmentation, encryption, logging, and workload protection.
- Document those patterns as standards that engineering teams can adopt.
- Review proposed designs, weighing cost and operational complexity against risk.
- Define baseline **guardrails** that let teams move quickly without stepping outside approved boundaries.
- Model threats against cloud-specific weaknesses, such as misconfigured permissions or exposed storage.
- Ensure the **shared responsibility model** is understood, so provider and organization duties are not confused.

## Skills and background

- **Platform depth:** deep familiarity with one or more major cloud platforms and the services they offer.
- **Technical foundations:** a solid grasp of identity systems, networking, cryptography, and infrastructure as code.
- **Automation at scale:** understanding how pipelines, templates, and policy-as-code tools enforce decisions across many teams.
- **Communication and judgment:** clear technical writing, the judgment to prioritize among competing risks, and the ability to align engineers, compliance teams, and leadership, since a design has value only when teams can implement it.

## Getting into the role

People commonly reach this role after experience in cloud engineering, security engineering, infrastructure, or systems administration, where they develop a feel for how cloud platforms behave under real conditions. Many build credibility through hands-on delivery and provider certifications before taking on architecture responsibilities; demonstrated design experience tends to carry particular weight.

## Career progression

With experience, an architect may grow into principal or enterprise architecture roles that span multiple platforms and business units, specialize in areas such as data protection or zero trust design, or move toward security leadership.

## Related roles

- [Cloud Security Engineer](cloud-security-engineer.md): builds and operates the controls the architect specifies.
- [Cloud Security Analyst](cloud-security-analyst.md): monitors environments and investigates activity against the architect's model.
- [Cloud Compliance Specialist](cloud-compliance-specialist.md): maps controls to regulatory and audit requirements.
- [DevSecOps Engineer](../application-security-roles/devsecops-engineer.md): embeds the architect's guardrails into the delivery pipeline so they are enforced as code ships.
