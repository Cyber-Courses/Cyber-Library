# Roles

Cybersecurity is not one job but a set of **functional areas**, each with its own focus, daily work, and expectations. Grouping roles this way helps a professional find where their interests and strengths fit, and it shows how the parts of a security program depend on one another. Many practitioners begin in one area and move across several over a career, and the boundaries between them shift from one organization to another.

## Role categories

- [Defensive Security Roles](defensive-security-roles/index.md): blue team work that monitors, investigates, and responds to threats, including the **SOC analyst** as a common entry point.
- [Offensive Security Roles](offensive-security-roles/index.md): authorized **adversarial testing**, such as penetration testing and red teaming, that surfaces weaknesses so defenders can fix them.
- [Forensics and IR Roles](forensics-and-ir-roles/index.md): investigation and response that reconstructs what happened, preserves **evidence**, and coordinates recovery.
- [Threat Intelligence Roles](threat-intelligence-roles/index.md): adversary-focused analysis that turns scattered data into **finished intelligence** for defenders and decision makers.
- [Application Security Roles](application-security-roles/index.md): building security into software close to engineering teams, from design through development and release.
- [Cloud Security Roles](cloud-security-roles/index.md): protecting data, workloads, and infrastructure in public, private, and hybrid cloud under the **shared responsibility model**.
- [Security Architecture Roles](security-architecture-roles/index.md): designing systems and controls so protection is a **designed property** rather than an afterthought.
- [IAM Roles](iam-roles/index.md): managing identities and access so the right people and systems hold the right access at the right time, and no more.
- [GRC Roles](grc-roles/index.md): governance, risk, and compliance work that aligns security with obligations, policy, and **risk tolerance**.
- [Privacy and Data Protection Roles](privacy-and-data-protection-roles/index.md): safeguarding **personal data** in line with privacy law such as the GDPR and sound practice.
