# Profile

Your **profile** is your working identity in security: the role you fill, the path you follow, the credentials you hold, and the skills you bring. Where Journey tracks movement over time and Landscape maps the surrounding ecosystem, Profile is about defining and developing yourself as a practitioner so your strengths match the work you want. The sections below set out the building blocks that profile is made of.

## What you will find

- [Roles and functional areas](roles/index.md): the distinct jobs across the field, from **security operations** and **incident response** to **offensive testing**, **governance**, and **architecture**, each with its own daily work and expectations.
- [Career paths and specialization tracks](career-paths/index.md): how roles connect over time, how a **generalist foundation** can branch into deep expertise, and how people move between **technical** and **leadership** directions.
- [Certifications and credentialing](certifications/index.md): the industry qualifications that **validate knowledge**, help with hiring screens, and signal commitment to a specialization.
- [Skill development](skill-development/index.md): the **technical abilities** that underpin the work and the **soft skills**, such as communication and judgment, that determine how far those abilities carry you.
