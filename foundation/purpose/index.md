# Purpose

Security is not an end in itself. It exists to let people and organizations pursue their goals safely in a world that runs on digital systems. This category steps back from the mechanics to ask a simpler question: what is all of this for? Answering it gives the rest of the library its direction. It belongs in the Foundation pillar because purpose shapes priorities, and knowing why security matters helps practitioners decide what to protect and how far to go.

The topics below set out the central aims.

**Protecting assets and people** covers the data, systems, and individuals that security exists to safeguard.

**Ensuring business continuity** keeps organizations running through disruption, failure, and attack.

**Enabling innovation securely** lets teams build and experiment without taking on unacceptable risk.

**Mitigating risk in a digital world** addresses the growing exposure that comes with connected technology.

**Fostering a resilient ecosystem** looks beyond any single system to the health and trustworthiness of the wider digital environment.
